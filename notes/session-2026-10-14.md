## Performance: connecting touching pieces

Optimized `connect_touching_pieces` in `physics.py`, measured on a 200-piece fracture with about 1185 touching pairs.

| Step | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Finding touching pairs | 0.111s | 0.028s |
| Whole function | ~0.22–0.28s | not re-measured (see below) |

What worked:
- Reading vertex positions once per piece with numpy (`foreach_get`), instead of through the Blender API for every pair.
- Checking all bounding boxes against each other in one numpy operation, then only running the expensive distance check on pairs whose boxes overlap.
- Only testing vertices that fall inside the other piece's bounding box.
- Building BVH trees from `loop_triangles` and `BVHTree.FromPolygons`, which takes 0.004s for all 200 pieces.
- Looking up each piece's collection once in `add_constraints`, instead of once per constraint.

What didn't work:
- `BVHTree.FromBMesh(epsilon=...)` with `overlap()`: found 0 pairs, because `epsilon` doesn't close the gap between pieces.
- Inflating the meshes and using `overlap()`: no faster, and found about 20 extra false pairs.
- Building BVH trees only when needed: no gain, because almost every piece overlaps a neighbour.

**Open:** the 0.104s total measured mid-session was invalid, because a bug meant no constraints were being registered. That bug is fixed, but the corrected total hasn't been timed yet.

## Constraint placement and orientation

- Each constraint empty is now placed at the centre of the face the two pieces share. Previously all empties sat at the world origin.
- Each empty is rotated to match its contact. The face's normal and long axis are fitted from the contact points with PCA, and the axes are mapped per constraint type:
  - **Hinge:** rotates around the face's long axis, through the face's centre. Moving it to the face's edge (door-style) was tried and then removed.
  - **Slider:** slides along the face.
  - **Piston, Generic and Generic Spring:** X points from one piece toward the other.
- Fixed a bug where hinges swivelled within the face's plane (a sheared look). The face normal was coming from side faces at the contact corners.
- Added fallbacks for corner-only contacts, where the normals can cancel out.

## New panel settings

- **Constraint Type:** Fixed, Point, Hinge, Slider, Piston, Generic, Generic Spring.
- **Collision Shape:** Convex Hull, Mesh, Box, Sphere, Capsule, Cylinder, Cone, Compound.
- **Body Type:** Dynamic or Passive.
- **Breaking Randomness and Breaking Seed:** vary each constraint's breaking threshold by up to ±the chosen fraction, repeatably.
- **Bake to Keyframes:** converts the simulation to keyframes over the rigid body cache's frame range.
- Collision margin now turns on Blender's custom margin automatically when it's above 0.

## Known issues

- **Crash on undo:** Blender 5.2 crashed (`EXCEPTION_ACCESS_VIOLATION` in `btRigidBody::removeConstraintRef`) when undoing with rigid body constraints in the scene. This is a Blender-side bug. Avoid Ctrl+Z after applying rigid bodies. Not yet checked against Blender's own Connect operator, or reported.
- **Square faces:** a perfectly square contact face has no longest direction, so its hinge axis may point along either edge. Fracture faces are rarely square.

Everything else above has been tested in Blender and works.
